## Prompt da casa

Create one production-ready game sprite on a genuinely transparent RGBA background: a cozy rabbit's woodland cottage for Craft Bunny, a warm 2D side-scrolling pixel-art platformer. House only, no characters, no scene, no sky, no ground rectangle, no text. Orthographic front/three-quarter side game prop. Rounded wooden arched door at ground level, tiny square warm windows, reddish orange tiled roof, brown timber and pale honey plaster, a few green leaves on roof edges, tiny carrot-shaped carving over door. Crisp hand-placed-looking pixel clusters, restrained 24-color palette, dark brown pixel outlines, simple readable silhouette at an on-screen size about 180 x 160 pixels. Flat grounded base across the bottom, transparent all around, no cast shadow below, no antialiasing, no blur, no photorealism. Center entire house with ample transparent margin. Save the full image; do not add labels or a sprite sheet.

## Prompt do galho

One reusable horizontal tree-branch platform sprite for Craft Bunny, a warm 2D pixel-art woodland platform game. Transparent RGBA background. A single very wide thick brown branch/log seen from the side, top surface absolutely horizontal and flat so a rabbit can walk on it. Left and right end edges are straight vertical cuts at exactly the same thickness, so the middle can be repeated to extend into long platforms. Small subtle green moss pixels only along upper surface. Detailed brown bark in warm orange and chocolate pixel clusters, dark lower outline, a few small knots, underside slightly uneven but no large branches sticking upwards. Aspect ratio about 6:1, keep thickness constant across most of the sprite. Entire platform isolated with transparent padding, no trees behind it, no ground, no leaves obscuring the walking surface, no characters, no text, no UI, no blur or smooth gradients. True crisp pixel-art style, readable at about 256 x 40 screen pixels.
